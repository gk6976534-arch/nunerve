NuNerve Ingredients

NuNerve™ is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to support nerve health and normal nervous system function. Its advertised formula contains eight key ingredients, including B vitamins, botanical extracts, and compounds studied for their potential roles in nerve function and antioxidant protection.

1

Whole Coffee Fruit Extract

Whole coffee fruit extract is obtained from the fruit surrounding coffee beans. It contains naturally occurring plant compounds and polyphenols. It has been studied for potential effects on brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), a protein involved in neuronal health. However, evidence that this ingredient in NuNerve repairs damaged nerves is insufficient.
2

Palmitoylethanolamide (PEA)

PEA is a fatty acid amide that occurs naturally in the body and is also available as a supplement. Researchers have studied it for its possible role in pain signaling and inflammatory responses. Findings are still developing, and its effectiveness can depend on the condition and formulation being studied.
3

Vitamin B1 (Benfotiamine)

Benfotiamine is a fat-soluble derivative of thiamine, also known as vitamin B1. Thiamine plays an essential role in energy metabolism and normal nerve function. Benfotiamine has been investigated in relation to diabetic nerve complications, but evidence of benefit is not conclusive for all patients.
4

Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxal-5-Phosphate)

Vitamin B6 helps the body produce neurotransmitters and supports normal nervous system function. Pyridoxal-5-phosphate is an active form of this vitamin. Adequate intake is important, but excessive vitamin B6 supplementation can itself cause nerve damage, so the dosage matters.
5

Vitamin B12 (Methylcobalamin)

Vitamin B12 is essential for normal nerve function, red blood cell formation, and DNA synthesis. Methylcobalamin is one form of this vitamin. Correcting a B12 deficiency can help address related neurological problems, but supplementation does not necessarily improve nerve symptoms when deficiency is absent.
6

R-Alpha Lipoic Acid (R-ALA)

Alpha-lipoic acid is a compound involved in cellular energy metabolism and has antioxidant properties. It has been studied for possible effects on symptoms associated with diabetic neuropathy. Research findings are mixed, and its benefits depend on the dose and specific formulation.
7

Lion's Mane Mushroom

Lion's Mane is an edible mushroom studied for its potential effects on neurological and cognitive functions. It contains compounds that have attracted interest in laboratory research involving nerve growth pathways. More human research is needed to determine whether supplementation meaningfully improves nerve health.
8

Organic Curcumin (Curcuma longa)

Curcumin is a naturally occurring compound found in turmeric. It has been investigated for antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Its absorption varies depending on the formulation, and there is insufficient evidence to conclude that curcumin in NuNerve regenerates damaged nerves.
